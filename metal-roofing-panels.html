Metal Roofing & Siding Panels

PBR panel, AG panel, and R-panel for roofs and walls, rolled on our own sheet-metal roller in Morrill. Pickup or delivery.

Panels we carry

  • PBR panel — 26 gauge.
  • AG panel — Ask for current gauge and colors.
  • R-panel — Ask for current gauge and colors.

PBR colors

White, Gray, Tan, Red, Blue, Black, Copper, and Dark green. Additional colors are available by order.

Ordering panels?

Send the profile, gauge, color, lengths, quantities, trim, location, required date, and pickup or delivery. Here’s what to send.

Pickup and delivery

Pick up at 30101 County Road 9, Morrill. Pickups follow normal business hours: Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM. Closed on federal holidays. Delivery is available roughly 250+ miles out, depending on the project.

For contractors

Contractor and volume pricing are available. Send an active or upcoming job through the Contractor Job Specification Review and we’ll review the actual job rather than give a generic number.
