Doors & Windows

Garage Doors & Windows

Ironworks is an authorized retailer of Midland Garage Doors and Gerkin Windows. Custom doors and windows can be ordered any time of year.

What we carry

  • Midland Garage Doors — Garage doors, ordered to your opening.
  • Gerkin Windows — Windows and doors, custom-ordered to your openings.
  • Garage door seals — Replacement seals. The right fit depends on the door type, existing setup, and measurements.

How to order

  • Measure the opening, or send the building plans.
  • Tell us the door or window style and color you want.
  • Call (308) 672-3891 and we’ll review the order with you.

Doors and windows are a regular, year-round product line, not a seasonal special.
