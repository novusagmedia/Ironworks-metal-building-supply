Custom Metal Trim & Hardware

Custom trim profiles rolled on our own sheet-metal roller, made to the spec your job needs. Fasteners and hardware too.

What we make and supply

  • Custom trim — Trim profiles made to your spec on our own roller.
  • Panel-matched trim — Trim for the panels you order, in the same color lineup: white, gray, tan, red, blue, black, copper, dark green.
  • Fasteners and hardware — The hardware to finish the job.

What to send

  • Each trim profile, with a sketch or dimensions if it’s custom
  • Color
  • Lengths and quantities
  • Project location and required date
  • Pickup or delivery

Not sure of a profile?

Send a photo or sketch and what it’s for. It’s easier to sort out on a call than to guess.

Pickup and delivery

Pickups follow normal business hours: Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM. Delivery is available roughly 250+ miles out, depending on the project.

Contractors: send the job through the Contractor Job Specification Review.
