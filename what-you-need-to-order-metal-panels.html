Panels & Trim

What do I need to order metal panels?

Nine details. Send the profile, gauge, color, lengths, quantities, trim, project location, required date, and whether you’ll pick up or need delivery. We review the actual job rather than give a generic number.

The nine details that make a panel job easy to review

  • Profile — Which panel. Ironworks carries PBR panel, AG panel, and R-panel.
  • Gauge — PBR panels are 26 gauge.
  • Color — Current PBR colors: white, gray, tan, red, blue, black, copper, dark green. Additional colors are available by order.
  • Lengths — The cut length of each panel run.
  • Quantities — How many panels at each length.
  • Trim and accessories — Every trim piece and accessory the job needs, with lengths.
  • Project location — City and state, so delivery and site conditions can be reviewed.
  • Required date — When the material needs to be on site.
  • Pickup or delivery — Whether you’ll collect the order in Morrill or need it delivered.

Missing a detail?

Send what you have and mark what’s still unknown. It’s easier to fill one gap on a call than to start over.

Picking up in Morrill

The shop is at 30101 County Road 9, Morrill, NE 69358. Pickups follow normal business hours: Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM. Closed on federal holidays.

Delivery

Delivery is available roughly 250+ miles out, depending on the project. Tell us the project location and required date, and we’ll confirm what works for your job.

Why no price list online? Pricing depends on the actual job. We do not want to guess at scope, timing, or price.
