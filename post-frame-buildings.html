Post-Frame Buildings

The most versatile system for ag, commercial, and residential. Strong, fast to build, and cost-effective across large spans.

What post-frame buildings are good for

  • Ag barns and shops
  • Equipment storage buildings
  • Personal shops and garages
  • Horse barns and hobby buildings
  • Small commercial projects

What Ironworks provides

  • Design — Lay it out in the 3D designer, then Spencer’s team reviews it with you.
  • Review for your location — Every project is reviewed for where it will stand: your site, your wind, your snow.
  • Materials — 26 gauge PBR panels in 8 colors, plus trim rolled on our own sheet-metal roller.
  • Installation — Ironworks can take the building from design through installation. Concrete is handled by our subcontractor partners.
  • Delivery — Delivery is available roughly 250+ miles out, depending on the project.

How to start

Start a Building Project Fit Check with your use, site, size, and timeline. For building projects, Spencer’s team calls to go over the details and then meets with you in person. Ironworks serves Nebraska, Wyoming, South Dakota, and northern Colorado.
